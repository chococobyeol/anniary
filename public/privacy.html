Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Anniary does not require an account. Your data stays in your browser unless you choose to export it or connect Google Drive for optional synchronization.

1. Data stored on your device

Anniary stores app data in your browser's local storage under the key anniary-storage. This may include boards, calendar entries, tasks, notes, ranges, tags, overlays, drawings, image stickers, settings, and view state.

Image stickers are processed and resized in your browser. A local JSON backup is created only when you choose to export one. The operator does not receive this locally stored content unless you separately enable Google Drive synchronization as described below.

2. Optional Google Drive synchronization

Google Drive connection is optional and is not required to use Anniary. If you connect it, Anniary requests permission to create and manage only the files that you open with or create through Anniary. It does not request access to all files in your Drive.

Anniary creates a visible Anniary folder and an anniary-data.json file in your Google Drive. This file can contain boards, entries, notes, drawings, image stickers, and synchronized settings. Synchronization traffic passes through Anniary's Cloudflare service but planner content is not retained in a separate operator database.

To keep the connection active, Anniary stores your Google account identifier and email, an encrypted Google authorization token, and a session record in Cloudflare D1. Google access tokens are not stored in your browser. Anniary uses Google information only to provide the synchronization feature you requested.

3. Other data and services

Anniary does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling. Cloudflare and Google may process ordinary technical request data, such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time, under their own privacy practices. Anniary does not keep a separate analytics copy of that information.

Anniary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Your control

You can remove individual content, use Erase all in Settings, or clear the site's data through your browser. Exported backup files remain under your control and must be deleted from the location where you saved them.

Disconnecting Google Drive removes Anniary's stored authorization and session records and revokes the connection. It does not delete your local planner or the visible backup in Google Drive. You can delete that file or the Anniary folder directly in Google Drive.

5. Security

Google authorization tokens are encrypted at the application layer and session cookies are restricted from JavaScript access. Protect your device and take care when storing or sharing exported and Drive backups. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if Anniary changes synchronization, adds analytics, or adds another feature that changes how information is handled. The effective date at the top shows the latest revision.
