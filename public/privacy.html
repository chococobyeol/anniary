Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Anniary does not require an account or collect your planner content. Your data stays in your browser unless you choose to export it.

1. Data stored on your device

Anniary stores app data in your browser's local storage under the key anniary-storage. This may include boards, calendar entries, tasks, notes, ranges, tags, overlays, drawings, image stickers, settings, and view state.

Image stickers are processed and resized in your browser. A JSON backup is created only when you choose to export one. The operator does not receive or have access to this locally stored content.

2. Data we do not collect

Anniary has no account system, server synchronization, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling. The app does not send your planner content or image stickers to the operator.

Like most websites, the hosting service may automatically handle ordinary request data, such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time, for delivery and security under its own privacy practices. Anniary does not keep a separate copy of that data.

3. Your control

You can remove individual content, use Erase all in Settings, or clear the site's data through your browser. Exported backup files remain under your control and must be deleted from the location where you saved them.

4. Security

Keeping planner content on your device limits its routine transmission, but local data is only as secure as your device and browser profile. Protect your device and take care when storing or sharing exported backups. No storage method is completely secure.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if Anniary adds an account, synchronization, analytics, or another feature that changes how information is handled. The effective date at the top shows the latest revision.
